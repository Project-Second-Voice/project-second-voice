Camino

Immigration education, practice, and preparedness.

Camino is a free educational and preparedness app focused on the United States immigration context. Learn key concepts, practice realistic situations, build a personal preparedness plan, and find reviewed public resources—all in one approachable place.

What you can do

Learn, rehearse, and prepare at your own pace.

Camino brings four connected tools together without promising outcomes or replacing qualified help.

01

Experiences

Practice difficult moments before they happen.

Work through interactive choices, feedback, and practice elements with adjustable support and difficulty.

  • Traffic Stop
  • At the Door
  • Workplace Encounter
  • Family Member Detained
  • Student Unsure of Status
02

Learn

Build practical understanding through active study.

Explore immigration concepts, rights education, preparedness, and source evaluation through quizzes, flashcards, matching activities, explanations, mixed review, and mastery tools.

03

Prepare

Turn preparedness into a plan you can revisit.

Create tasks, trusted contacts, notes, document-location reminders, family and transportation plans, questions for legal help, and support planning.

Important: Camino records notes about where documents are kept. It is not secure document storage and does not upload document files.

04

Help

Find reviewed public resources.

Search by need, location, situation, language, or service type. Resource links open the provider’s own website, where current availability and privacy practices are controlled by that provider.

Privacy by design

Your plans stay with you.

Camino requires no account. Learning progress, settings, scenario progress, and preparedness-plan entries are stored locally on the device. The app has no advertising or cross-app tracking, and practice reminders are scheduled locally through iOS.

The installed app requests its visual font from Google Fonts, which receives technical request data such as the device’s IP address and user-agent information. Camino does not include planner entries, contacts, notes, or learning progress in that request. External Help websites have their own privacy practices.

Camino support

Help with the app, resources, privacy, or access.

Email Project Second Voice at projectsecondvoice@gmail.com. Support is available in English; messages in Spanish or Simplified Chinese are also welcome.

Before you email

  1. Close and reopen Camino.
  2. Confirm your iPhone is running the latest iOS version available to it.
  3. For an external Help link, check your internet connection and try opening the provider site again.
  4. For reminders, review Camino’s reminder setting and the iOS notification permission for Camino.

What to include

Use the subject Camino Support and include:

  • the screen or feature involved
  • what you expected and what happened
  • steps that reproduce the issue
  • your iPhone model, iOS version, Camino version, and selected app language

Please do not email immigration documents, case or A-numbers, government IDs, medical records, passwords, exact addresses, or other sensitive personal information.

Common questions

Camino FAQ

Does Camino provide legal advice?

No. Camino provides general education, practice, preparedness tools, and public-resource links. Personal legal questions need a qualified attorney or accredited representative.

Do I need an account?

No. Camino has no account, sign-in, or authentication system.

Does Camino work offline?

Its built-in learning, scenario, and planning content is stored with the app and can be used without a connection. External Help websites and email support require internet access. The app also requests its typeface from Google Fonts when network access is available.

Where is my information stored?

Progress, preferences, and information entered into Prepare are stored locally in Camino’s app storage on the device. Camino has no backend account or remote database for this information.

Why does a Help resource open another website?

Camino is a directory, not the provider of those third-party services. Opening a resource takes you to the provider’s website, which operates independently under its own terms and privacy practices.

How can I report an outdated resource?

Email Project Second Voice with the resource name, the page or URL, and the detail that appears outdated. Do not include personal case information.

What languages does Camino support?

The app interface and core Camino content support English, Spanish, and Simplified Chinese. External provider websites set their own language availability.
