About the platform

A serious home for stories that deserve to last.

Project Second Voice is a youth-led storytelling initiative dedicated to documenting immigrant experiences through digital media. The platform creates space for reflection, memory, and empathy while honoring the nuance behind each voice.

By pairing thoughtful storytelling with accessible digital formats, the project helps communities, institutions, and new audiences engage with immigrant experiences as deeply human narratives.

Community partnership

Building immigrant advocacy and youth organizing within San Diego communities.

Project Second Voice is developing a partnership with United We Dream to help start a local chapter in the Poway Unified and San Diego area, connecting immigrant youth advocacy with storytelling, awareness, and community organizing.

The collaboration is rooted in youth leadership: helping students and families share their experiences, build advocacy skills, and turn stories into meaningful local action.
