Community partnerships

We work with community organizations to connect immigrant storytelling with youth leadership, education, and advocacy.

Featured partner

United We Dream

United We Dream is the largest immigrant youth-led network in the United States. It supports undocumented immigrants and young people through grassroots organizing, leadership development, advocacy, and public campaigns for systemic reform.

Partnership goals

A local chapter in San Diego

We’re working to establish a United We Dream chapter in Poway Unified and the San Diego area. It would give students and families a place to organize, learn about immigrant rights, and build advocacy skills.

  • Make organizing more accessible to immigrant youth.
  • Connect stories from the archive with sustained local advocacy.
  • Build support that responds to the needs of local families and neighborhoods.

Civil rights education

As part of the United We Dream partnership, Project Second Voice also connects visitors to a student-led civil rights education project created by Jonathan Wang. The project introduces United We Dream's history and mission, explains the challenges facing Dreamers, DACA recipients, undocumented youth, and mixed-status families, and frames immigrant advocacy as part of a broader civil rights conversation.

The initiative pairs research on policy, legal access, deportation defense, and citizenship pathways with reflective writing about bias and belonging. That combination mirrors the goals of Project Second Voice: stories do more than document harm; they help communities understand why advocacy, education, and youth leadership matter.

Advocacy initiative

DREAM Act Awareness & Advocacy Campaign

Through Project Second Voice and our broader advocacy efforts, we create educational campaigns designed to help students understand immigration legislation and civic issues that directly affect immigrant communities. This DREAM Act awareness campaign was created to educate students on how the legislation could impact Dreamers and immigrant families while encouraging informed civic engagement and advocacy.

The DREAM Act aims to create a pathway toward legal status and protection for undocumented youth who grew up in the United States. Many Dreamers have spent the majority of their lives contributing to their communities, attending schools, and building futures in the country they call home. Through education and awareness, Project Second Voice hopes to encourage understanding and constructive discussion surrounding legislation affecting immigrant communities.

First Gen Empower

First Gen Empower expands educational opportunity for undocumented, first-generation, and low-income students. The nonprofit offers training, workshops, consulting, and public resources for educators, counselors, and institutions.

Programs and support

  • Educator training
  • Undocu-Ally workshops
  • BRIDGE certification
  • Institutional consulting
  • Student workshops
  • Advising guides
  • Mixed-status family resources
  • Career resources
  • Educational resources
  • Policy resources
  • First-generation stories

Why we’re sharing these resources

We’re highlighting First Gen Empower to help students, families, and educators find support alongside the stories they read here.
